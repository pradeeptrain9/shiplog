ShipLog

Turn your Claude Code transcript and a spoken note into your weekly check-in, build-in-public post, submission write-up and demo script. Runs fully in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

Where do I find transcripts?

Claude Code saves each session as a .jsonl file. On macOS/Linux, list the newest with ls -t ~/.claude/projects/*/*.jsonl | head. You can also paste plain text below (one instruction per line) and ShipLog will treat each line as a prompt.

Polish with AI (optional, off by default)

When on, your masked draft outputs (stats, your note, up to 5 quoted prompts) go to the chosen provider. The raw transcript is never sent. Free tiers may log or train on inputs; check the provider's terms.

Word counts use your prompts to Claude as a proxy for words dictated (pasted blocks are excluded); the official number is your Wispr Insights card. Likely secrets (API keys, tokens, emails) and your home folder name are masked in the output, but read it before posting. Your input never leaves this page; the mic uses your browser's speech service, which may send audio to its provider.
